ABOUT MALIBUDGET

Budgeting should help you feel more prepared — not judged.

MaliBudget is a household budgeting website designed around the real costs people plan for every day: groceries, electricity, transport, school needs, bills and unexpected expenses.

The goal is not to tell people what they should spend. It is to help people understand their available money and make a plan that works for their situation.

  • Simple, practical money planning
  • A clearer view of bills and spending
  • Designed with everyday households in mind
